What we believe

Software should solve a problem, not introduce three more and a subscription. We build practical tools with a preference for clarity, simplicity and getting out of your way.

The founder

Founded by Krish, a web developer with a finite supply of time and an apparently infinite supply of things to fix. The studio exists to turn those annoyances into useful projects.

About the name
