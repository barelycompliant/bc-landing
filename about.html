Why we exist

Barely ⊅ Compliant began in October 2026 as a place to share vibe-coded tools that might be useful to other people. It is a hobby with professional standards, not a venture capital pitch.

A note from the founder

Krish / Founder & Developer

I started programming when I was eight, beginning with Scratch and eventually moving to Python. I tried building Android apps and working with microcontrollers to make electronic trinkets. Other commitments eventually caught up with me, and these days I work with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

I'm a student, and outside of software I enjoy sailing and working on cars. I started Barely Compliant to share things I'd built with AI that other people might actually find useful.

I'd like people to discuss AI with more nuance. There are legitimate concerns about its environmental impact, reliability and use, but treating every application of AI as identical doesn't help anyone understand those concerns.

Software should have a sensible price

I believe developers should benefit from their work. I also prefer perpetual licensing for software that has no ongoing operating costs. Not everything needs to be a subscription. Eventually I'd like to build reasonably priced physical products for people who simply have a problem to solve. At the moment, low production volumes would make that difficult.

The name

⊅ means “not a superset of.” It is barely compliant with our name. How fitting! Plus, it looks cool.
