/04 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Some of these questions may not have been asked frequently. We answered them anyway.

What do you actually do?

We solve annoying problems with software. Occasionally the solution is useful to somebody else, so we publish it.

Are you a real business?

Barely Compliant is a hobby with professional standards, founded in October 2026.

What does ⊅ mean?

Not a superset of. It is barely compliant with our name. How fitting. It also looks cool.

Is everything free?

Daybook and BingBeGone are free and open source. Future projects may be paid. Developers deserve compensation for their work.

Will everything eventually become a subscription?

We prefer perpetual licenses for software without ongoing costs. Recurring costs can justify recurring fees; the mere existence of a billing platform does not.

Are you making physical products?

That is a future goal. Low production volumes currently make reasonable pricing difficult.

Can I hire you?

Possibly in the future. We are not advertising commissioned work right now.

It doesn’t work.

That is unfortunate. Which product? What did you do? What happened? Email us those details and we have something to work with.

Do you use AI?

Yes. There is an entire page about it. We have apparently reached that point.
