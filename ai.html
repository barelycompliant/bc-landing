/03 — AI POLICY

About the AI thing.

Apparently, this needs its own page now.

Do you use AI to make your software?

Yes.

Seriously?

Yes. That was not a typo.

But aren’t you web developers?

We are. We also have other commitments. We use ChatGPT and Claude for code, debugging, documentation, design, branding and writing. Sometimes they help with a function; sometimes they generate substantial portions of a project.

So you just vibe code everything and hit publish?

We vibe code. We do not vibe deploy. Every line of code is reviewed by a human for obvious bugs. We test functionality, follow the flow of the code and inspect execution traces. When practical, the founder uses the software day to day before release.

Does that mean there are no bugs?

No. Humans have been producing bugs quite successfully without AI for decades.

I write every line of my code myself.

Okay.

I don’t even use autocomplete.

That sounds like a lot of typing.

Is AI going to replace every programmer?

We were hoping to finish our school planner first.

Does asking for a brownie recipe drain a small village’s water supply?

AI has real energy and water costs. They vary by model, infrastructure and workload. A sweeping claim about every AI request is not a useful substitute for evidence. Neither is pretending the costs don’t exist.

What about privacy and licensing?

Those are legitimate concerns. AI output can contain errors or problematic material, and inputs may be handled by third-party providers. Human review matters, but it is not a guarantee of security or licensing compliance.

Do Daybook and BingBeGone use AI when I run them?

No. AI helped build them. Neither requires an AI model at runtime.

Did AI help write this page?

Yes. Imagine lying about that here, of all places.

I really can’t stand that you use AI and need to yell at somebody, can I yell at you?

Knock yourself out.

Can I report an actual bug too?

Please do. Email support@barelycompliant.com with the product, browser or operating system, and steps to reproduce it.

I can’t stand that you use AI.

Then don’t use our stuff, this just ain’t that complicated.

Are we done?

Ideally.
